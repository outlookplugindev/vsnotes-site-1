💼 AI Job Roles in 2025
🧠
ML Engineer
$120K – $250K / year
Build and deploy machine learning models in production. Bridge between data science and software engineering. Design ML pipelines, optimize model inference, monitor production systems.
🔥 Demand
Python PyTorch MLOps Docker

AI/LLM Engineer
$130K – $280K / year
The hottest role right now. Build RAG systems, fine-tune LLMs, create agents and workflows using LangChain, Semantic Kernel, or custom solutions.

Data Scientist
$100K – $200K / year
Analyze data to extract business insights, build predictive models, conduct A/B tests, create dashboards. Heavy on statistics and storytelling with data.
📈 High Demand
Python SQL Statistics Tableau

AI Research Scientist
$150K – $400K / year
Push the frontiers of AI — new architectures, training methods, alignment research. Typically at FAANG, AI labs, or top universities. Requires PhD or equivalent.

MLOps Engineer
$110K – $210K / year
DevOps for ML — build CI/CD for models, monitor drift, manage infrastructure (Kubernetes, cloud). Ensure ML models stay healthy in production.
⚙️ Growing Fast
Kubernetes MLflow AWS/GCP

Prompt Engineer
$80K – $175K / year
Design and optimize prompts for LLM-powered applications. Part art, part science. Strong writing + technical understanding required. Entry point for non-coders.

🗺️ 12-Month AI Career Roadmap
1
📖 Learn Python Fundamentals
Variables, functions, OOP, Pandas, NumPy. Use freeCodeCamp, Python.org, or CS50P.
Month 1–2
2
📊 Master Machine Learning
Scikit-learn, supervised/unsupervised learning, model evaluation. Andrew Ng's ML Specialization is the gold standard.
Month 2–4
3
🧠 Deep Learning & Neural Nets
PyTorch, CNNs, RNNs, Transformers. Deep Learning Specialization by Andrew Ng, Fast.ai.
Month 3–5
4
💬 LLMs & Prompt Engineering
OpenAI API, Anthropic API, prompt patterns, fine-tuning basics, tokenization deep dive.
Month 4–6
5
🔍 Build RAG Systems
LangChain or Semantic Kernel, FAISS/Chroma, build a document QA app. Follow this site's tutorials!
Month 5–7
6
⚡ Agentic AI Projects
Build agents with LangGraph or AutoGen. Tool-calling, multi-step reasoning, memory systems.
Month 6–8
7
🚀 Build a Portfolio (3 Projects)
RAG chatbot, ML web app, Agentic AI tool. Deploy on HuggingFace Spaces, Streamlit Cloud, or Vercel.
Month 7–10
8
📜 Get Certified
AWS ML Specialty, Google Cloud Professional ML Engineer, or DeepLearning.AI certificates.
Month 8–11
9
🎯 Apply, Network, and Land the Job
Optimize LinkedIn, contribute to open-source, write technical blogs, reach out to recruiters.
Month 10–12

💡 CAREER PRO TIPS
  • Build in public. Share your projects on GitHub and LinkedIn. AI hiring is highly portfolio-driven.
  • Start with LLM Engineering. It's the fastest-growing, highest-paying entry point in 2025 — no PhD needed.
  • Contribute to open-source. LangChain, HuggingFace, Ollama — even small PRs build credibility.
  • Write about what you learn. A blog post explaining RAG gets you more interviews than a certificate.
  • Learn the business side. AI engineers who understand why a model matters to the business are 10× more valuable.
  • Network at AI meetups. Most jobs are still filled through connections. Attend local and virtual AI events.
  • Don't wait until you're "ready." Start applying when you have 2–3 solid projects. You'll learn more on the job.
